# Binary Contrasts

The "It's not X. It's Y." construction is a false-choice rhetorical flourish that manufactures drama out of a claim that didn't need one.

- "It's not X. It's Y." | severity: 8
- "Not a tool. A revolution." | severity: 9
- "This isn't just [X]. It's [Y]." | severity: 8
- "It's not about X, it's about Y." | severity: 6
- "X isn't the point. Y is." | severity: 6
