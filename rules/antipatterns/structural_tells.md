# Structural Tells

Sentence-shape and paragraph-shape habits, not individual words. Drawn from
no-ai-slop's colon-reveal and synonym-cycling categories. Synonym cycling
(restating the same point several ways in a row) requires comparing the
meaning of multiple sentences, not just matching text, so it isn't
pattern-matchable and stays a `/clearfelt rewrite` reasoning step instead
of a rule bullet here; see "Qualitative signals" in `reference/audit.md`.
