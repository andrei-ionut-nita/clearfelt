# Frictionless Claims

Phrases that describe a result as costless: no friction, no risk, nothing debatable behind it. AI-generated prose tends to resolve every problem cleanly and skip the part where something was actually hard or contested. The regex-detectable subset lives here. The fuller version of this test, whether the piece admits any real stakes anywhere, isn't pattern-matchable and stays a `/clearfelt humanize` reasoning step; see "Qualitative signals" in `reference/audit.md`.
