# Confidence Inflation Lexicon

Phrases that claim certainty, completeness, or superiority the surrounding text hasn't actually earned, distinct from `vague_abstraction_lexicon.md` (which substitutes a vague word for a concrete detail) and `puffery_lexicon.md` (marketing hype words). This category is specifically about overclaiming: "impossible to overstate" and "guaranteed to" assert a degree of certainty no single sentence can actually establish. Deliberately multi-word phrases here, not standalone words like "always" or "never": those are ordinary intensifiers in everyday human writing, and flagging them would punish normal speech, not AI overclaiming, see writing.md's "formality is not a flaw" doctrine, the same reasoning that keeps this category from listing single common words.
