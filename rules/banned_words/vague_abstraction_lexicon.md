# Vague Abstraction Lexicon

Words that stand in for a concrete detail instead of naming it. Distinct from the high-frequency AI lexicon (unusual single words like "delve") and the puffery lexicon (marketing hype): these are ordinary-sounding words that let a sentence read as informative without committing to a fact, a number, or a name.
